How readiness is estimated

Every learning day is a sample. Conversations seen on only one or two days reveal how many legitimate conversations have not shown up yet (Chao2 estimator), and how many will appear in the next days (Shen-Chao-Lin extrapolation). Ranges come from a parametric bootstrap and are backtested on this site's own history.
